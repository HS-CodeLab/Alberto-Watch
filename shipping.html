IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Before your order arrives.

Helpful things to know about delivery arrangements.

Delivery Areas

Available destinations depend on courier coverage and your delivery address. Please contact support to confirm whether delivery is available in your area before ordering.

Estimated Delivery Time

Delivery estimates can vary by destination, stock availability, order processing and courier conditions. Confirm the current estimate when placing your order.

Shipping Charges

Any applicable delivery fee should be communicated during checkout or order confirmation. Do not assume free delivery unless it is explicitly offered.

Address Accuracy

Provide your complete address, city and reachable phone number. If you notice a mistake, contact support promptly and include your order reference.

Please note: Shipping fees, coverage, courier partners and delivery timelines are not fixed on this informational page. Confirm the details for your particular order with ALBERTO CLOCK before purchase.
